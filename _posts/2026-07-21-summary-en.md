## [EU Fines Aliexpress €550 Million Over Counterfeit Goods](https://thebalkanchronicle.com/en/business/eu-fines-aliexpress-550-million-counterfeit-goods-2026/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The European Commission fined Aliexpress €550 million on July 20, 2025, for failing to effectively prevent counterfeit goods on its platform, and mandated a remediation plan by October 20, 2026. This is one of the largest fines under the Digital Services Act, setting a strong precedent for platform accountability regarding illegal and counterfeit goods in the EU. The fine followed a 2024 investigation under the DSA, which found that unsafe products like toys and cosmetics remained online for weeks after being flagged. Aliexpress called the penalty &quot;disproportionate&quot; and plans to review its options.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 21, 01:44

**Background**: The Digital Services Act \(DSA\) is an EU regulation that took effect in 2022, requiring online platforms to implement robust content moderation and transparency measures. It aims to combat illegal content, counterfeit goods, and other harmful activities online. The DSA applies to all platforms operating in the EU, with large platforms facing stricter obligations.
