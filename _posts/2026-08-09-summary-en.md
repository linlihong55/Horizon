## [Generative AI designs viable phage genomes with Evo 1 and Evo 2](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers used the genome language models Evo 1 and Evo 2 to generate complete bacteriophage genomes, and experimental testing confirmed 16 novel viable phages with realistic genetic architectures and host tropism. This marks the first generative design of functional whole genomes. This is the first demonstration that AI can generate not just short sequences but entire viable genomes, opening new possibilities for synthetic biology and AI-driven genomics. The ability to design functional phages could accelerate targeted antibacterial therapies and deepen our understanding of genome evolution. The study used the lytic phage ΦX174 as the design template and leveraged the Evo 1 and Evo 2 genome language models, which are trained on vast genomic sequence libraries. The 16 experimentally validated phages showed substantial evolutionary novelty, while also exhibiting realistic genetic architectures and host specificity.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

**Background**: Genome language models \(gLMs\) are deep learning models pretrained on DNA and RNA sequences, analogous to large language models for text, and are capable of generating and predicting genomic sequences. While gLMs have achieved state-of-the-art results in variant effect prediction and short-sequence design, generating an entire functional genome has remained untested. This study bridges that gap by using gLMs to design complete bacteriophage genomes that are experimentally verified as viable.

## [W3C&\#x27;s 1998 &\#x27;Cool URIs Don&\#x27;t Change&\#x27; Still Resonates on Hacker News](https://www.w3.org/Provider/Style/URI) ⭐️ 8.0/10

This Hacker News thread resurfaced Tim Berners-Lee&\#x27;s 1998 W3C essay &\#x27;Cool URIs Don&\#x27;t Change,&\#x27; with commenters sharing live examples of broken government links and modern redirect-driven URL management. The discussion shows that 28 years later, stable URL design remains an unresolved challenge. Even official links from major institutions fail, making link rot a persistent threat to web archives, citations, and user trust. The thread highlights that while SEO and CMS redirects have alleviated the problem, long-term URI stability still depends on deliberate organizational discipline. The original essay defines a cool URI as one that does not change, noting &\#x27;URIs don&\#x27;t change: people change them.&\#x27; Commenters cite an NSF.gov 1998 publication returning HTTP 404 and Microsoft&\#x27;s own support links landing on generic pages as contemporary counterexamples.

hackernews · Klaster\_1 · Aug 9, 14:32 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49231809)

**Background**: A URI \(Uniform Resource Identifier\) is a string that identifies a resource, while a URL \(Uniform Resource Locator\) is a URI that also specifies how to locate and retrieve it. Link rot is the tendency of hyperlinks to stop working over time as pages move or disappear without redirection, a problem Wikipedia and most large websites also face. Tim Berners-Lee, the inventor of the Web, published this guidance on the W3C site to encourage designers to build stable identifiers.

## [World&\#x27;s largest single AI compute facility goes live in Inner Mongolia](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367666.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 6, Envision Group officially put into operation its Yuanjing Ulanqab Xinghe Base in Inner Mongolia, the world&\#x27;s largest single AI computing facility. The base has a planned total capacity of 2GW, uses over 80% green power, and is designed to support million-GPU parallel computing. This milestone signals a major expansion of China&\#x27;s AI infrastructure capacity and validates the scalability of large-scale, green-powered AI clusters. It also strengthens the national &\#x27;East Data, West Computing&\#x27; strategy by placing massive compute capacity in an energy-rich region near Beijing. The base covers 120,000 square meters \(about 20 standard football fields\) and is located in Ulanqab, one of the eight national &\#x27;East Data, West Computing&\#x27; hub nodes, about 240 km from Beijing with a data transmission latency of 4.2 ms. It is the first flagship project of Envision&\#x27;s &\#x27;Mission Gobi&\#x27; plan, aiming to provide a replicable blueprint for domestic computing clusters.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 9, 05:06

**Background**: &\#x27;East Data, West Computing&\#x27; \(Dong Shu Xi Suan\) is a Chinese national project launched in 2022 to build an integrated national data-center system, channeling compute-intensive workloads from eastern coastal regions to western regions with abundant energy and cooler climates. Ulanqab is one of the eight national hub nodes, and its data-center electricity prices are about 50% lower than those in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei area. Envision&\#x27;s &\#x27;Mission Gobi&\#x27; plan, also called GobiX, aims to address AI-driven power shortages by deploying large-scale AI clusters in the Gobi and other western regions.

## [Musk Unveils Robotic Lunar Factory Plan to Build AI Satellites](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/pure-insanity-elon-musk-details-173635969.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

In SpaceX&\#x27;s first public earnings call, Elon Musk outlined a plan to build an automated factory on the Moon. Starship rockets would deliver equipment, robots would extract metals from lunar soil, and an electromagnetic mass driver would launch AI satellites into orbit. The announcement signals SpaceX&\#x27;s long-term ambition to shift heavy manufacturing off Earth and could reshape the economics of AI satellite deployment. If realized, it would make lunar resources a foundation for orbital infrastructure. The factory would use in-situ resource utilization to extract aluminum, titanium, and silicon from lunar soil. The plan faces extreme challenges, including abrasive lunar dust, 14-day day/night cycles, and the company&\#x27;s current financial loss of $205 million in its space division.

**Background**: A mass driver is a proposed non-rocket launch system that uses electromagnetic coils to accelerate payloads to orbital velocities. In-situ resource utilization \(ISRU\) refers to using materials available at a destination, like lunar regolith, instead of hauling them from Earth. Lunar dust is an especially difficult problem: as NASA notes, it clings to equipment and can cause overheating and abrasion.
