## [OpenAI&\#x27;s Jalapeño ASIC Outperforms Nvidia Blackwell in Inference](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/openai-jalapeno-better-than-nvidia) ⭐️ 9.0/10

OpenAI published first test results for its self-designed inference ASIC &\#x27;Jalapeño,&\#x27; claiming it delivers 1.5–1.9× more AI work per watt, 1.7–3.6× lower latency, and 2.1–4.1× higher performance in high-interaction scenarios versus Nvidia&\#x27;s GB300 \(Blackwell\) on models like GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek R1 670B, and Kimi K2.5 1T. The chip, developed with Broadcom, is slated for deployment in OpenAI&\#x27;s own data centers by the end of the year. This challenges Nvidia&\#x27;s dominance in AI inference hardware by showing a custom ASIC can beat a top-tier GPU on both performance and total cost of ownership \(TCO\). It signals that large AI labs may increasingly design their own silicon, reshaping the AI hardware market and potentially lowering inference costs broadly. The chip is rated at 700W but sustains no more than 550W in practice. Benchmarks were run against Nvidia&\#x27;s GB300 \(Blackwell\) and did not include Nvidia&\#x27;s recently shipping Vera Rubin; Jalapeño is also not intended for model training. OpenAI says a second-generation chip is in advanced development and a third is being designed.

hackernews · Semianalysis · Aug 25, 14:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49434378)

**Background**: An ASIC \(application-specific integrated circuit\) is a chip custom-designed for a particular workload, unlike general-purpose GPUs. TCO \(total cost of ownership\) accounts for acquisition plus energy, maintenance, and other lifecycle costs, which is why efficiency per watt matters. Nvidia&\#x27;s Blackwell architecture is a GPU microarchitecture used in data-center accelerators like the GB300; it succeeded Hopper and is a current benchmark for AI inference and training hardware.

Vera CPU handles CPU-side work such as code execution, tool use, sandboxing, and orchestration, while Groq 3 LPX is an inference accelerator that combines GPU and LPU strengths for low-latency token generation.

It supports both lossy and lossless compression, offering significantly better compression efficiency than legacy JPEG while supporting wide color gamut, high dynamic range, and animation. The format was designed as a long-term successor to JPEG/JFIF.

This is one of the largest and most complex Python 2-to-3 migrations ever undertaken, and it will serve as a valuable real-world case study for other organizations still running legacy Python code. The outcome could influence how the community approaches large-scale migrations and how Stackless-dependent projects plan their futures. The game last upgraded its runtime in 2010 to Stackless Python 2.7, which is now long past its end-of-life. Futurize is a widely used automated tool that applies 2to3 fixers and adds compatibility imports to help migrate codebases from Python 2 to Python 3.
